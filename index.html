What is the Jago?

A celebration of
joy & blessings

The Jago is a beautiful pre-wedding tradition in Punjabi culture — a lively night of singing, dancing and blessings led by the women of the family. It's one of the most joyful and energetic celebrations of the wedding festivities.

The word "Jago" means "wake up" — traditionally, the women carry a decorated pot (the Jago pot) lit from within and sing songs to announce the wedding is approaching. Expect colour, noise, laughter and lots of dancing.

This is the evening that sets the tone for everything that follows — come with your dancing shoes on and your singing voice ready.

The Evening

What to expect

Singing & Music

Traditional Punjabi folk songs fill the room — everyone joins in. Live music and joyful noise are the order of the evening.

Dancing

Bhangra, Giddha and everything in between. The dancefloor will be open all evening — this is the night the celebrations truly begin.

Blessings & Tradition

The Jago pot — decorated and illuminated — is carried by the family as a symbol of celebration and blessings for the couple.

Day Two

A day of colour,
art & music

The second day brings two of the most beloved pre-wedding celebrations — the Mehndi, where intricate henna designs are applied to the bride, and the Sangeet, an evening of music, dance and performances.

The Mehndi is a beautiful and intimate ritual held together. For the Sangeet, the groom's side and bride's side will each be hosting their own separate celebration — full details are to be confirmed and will be shared with guests soon.

The Celebrations

Mehndi & Sangeet

Part One · Afternoon

The Mehndi

Henna artists will be applying intricate mehndi designs to the bride and guests. The Mehndi is a time for sitting together, chatting, laughing and admiring the beautiful art being created. It is said the darker the mehndi, the deeper the love — so stay patient and let it dry!

All ladies are encouraged to have their mehndi done. Light refreshments will be served.

Part Two · Evening

The Sangeet

As evening arrives, the Sangeet begins — a joyful celebration of music, dance and performances. This year, both families are hosting their own separate Sangeet events. Full details are to be confirmed, but come ready for Bollywood, Bhangra and everything in between.

The Big Day

The Anand Karaj

The Anand Karaj — meaning "Blissful Union" — is the Sikh wedding ceremony. Jeevan and Nikita will be joined in marriage through four sacred rounds (Lavaan) of the Guru Granth Sahib, each representing a stage of their journey together.

The ceremony is deeply spiritual and joyful, conducted in the presence of the Guru Granth Sahib. Tissues are very much encouraged.

Following the ceremony, the day continues with lunch, relaxation, before the evening reception at the spectacular Jardins d'Alfàbia.

About the Ceremony

The four Lavaan

Lavan One

Setting Out

The first round calls upon the couple to embrace the path of righteousness and begin their journey together with God's grace.

Lavan Two

Divine Love

The second round focuses on love — the love between husband and wife reflecting the divine love between a soul and God.

Lavan Three

Detachment

The third round brings growing detachment from the material world and a deepening spiritual connection between the couple.

Lavan Four

Blissful Union

The final round — the Anand Karaj is complete. The couple is now joined in a blissful, eternal union. This is the moment.

Practical Info

Good to know

Getting Around

Taxis are readily available and affordable. Bolt and Cabify apps work well. The hotel is 3km from the airport — a 10-minute taxi.

Weather in May

Late May brings 22–27°C with long sunny days and very little rain. Evenings are warm — bring a light layer for outdoor events. Sun cream is essential.

Language & Money

Catalan and Spanish are spoken locally. English is widely spoken in tourist areas. Currency is Euros (€). Most places accept card.

Food & Drink

Don't miss pa amb oli, sobrasada, ensaïmada and fresh seafood. Dining is later than in the UK — 9pm is normal for dinner.

Swimming

The sea in late May is around 20°C. Playa de Palma is right outside the hotel. Es Trenc is the most spectacular beach for swimming.
